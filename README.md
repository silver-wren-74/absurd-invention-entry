Entry for the Make.fun competition "One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch".

`main.py` writes a single sentence (220 characters max) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Introducing the Sock Reunion Service: a washing machine that, the moment it swallows one sock, prints a missing poster for it and goes on laundry strike until the pair is reunited.

The pitch fixes the tiny everyday annoyance of the vanished sock by turning
the culprit itself into the detective: the machine that ate the sock must
poster for it and strike until its victim comes home.
